# **ONE-PAGE EXECUTIVE & DELIVERY SUMMARY**

### **SHA-Aligned Digital Health Insurance Platform**

---

## **What We Are Building**

We are building a **national-scale digital platform** that allows **SHA, private insurers, healthcare providers, and patients** to operate on a **single, trusted system** for healthcare coverage and payments.

The platform replaces physical insurance cards with a **secure digital identity** and manages the **entire healthcare financing process**, from patient verification to provider payment.

---

## **What the Platform Will Do**

### **For Patients**

* Hold a **digital insurance identity** instead of a physical card  
* Verify coverage instantly at any accredited facility  
* Receive care without paperwork delays

### **For Healthcare Providers**

* Verify SHA coverage in real time  
* Submit claims digitally  
* Track approvals and payments transparently  
* Reduce fraud and manual follow-ups

### **For SHA & Insurers**

* Control member registration and coverage rules  
* Enforce benefit limits automatically  
* Monitor claims, payments, and provider behavior  
* Maintain a full audit trail for accountability

---

## **How the Platform Is Structured (Simple View)**

The system is built as **independent but connected services**, each responsible for one function:

* **Member Management** – who is covered  
* **Provider Management** – who can deliver services  
* **Coverage & Eligibility** – what SHA covers and when  
* **Care Encounters** – when care is delivered  
* **Claims Processing** – what is billed and approved  
* **Payments & Reconciliation** – how providers are paid  
* **Documents & Records** – supporting evidence  
* **Audit & Compliance** – who did what and when

This structure allows the system to:

* Scale gradually  
* Be updated without downtime  
* Remain stable even under high national usage

## **How Delivery Will Be Managed**

The work is divided into **clear phases**, each producing usable outcomes:

1. **Foundation Phase**  
   * Member, provider, and SHA setup

2. **Transaction Phase**

   * Payments

3. **Scale & Integration Phase**

Each phase can be budgeted, tracked, and approved independently.
